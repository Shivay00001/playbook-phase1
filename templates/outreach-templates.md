## 4. Pilot close script (on the call — from playbook 21.2, with real pricing)

> "Here's what I propose: a Starter Agent Sprint at [₹35,000 / $3,500 — match currency]. I build one agent that [agreed outcome — e.g. replies to every lead in under 60 seconds and books site visits].
>
> You pay 50% to start. I deliver in 14 days. If it doesn't hit [agreed metric] in 30 days, I fix it free or refund you.
>
> We can start Monday. Does that work?"

**Objection handling:**
- *Price* → "One receptionist costs you [₹25k/mo / $3k/mo]. This is a one-time install that works 24/7 and never takes leave. Here's the ROI math for your lead volume…"
- *Trust* → "Fair. That's why there's a 30-day outcome guarantee — and here are the working demos you can click through right now: [links]."
- *Timing* → "That's exactly why it's a 14-day install. We start Monday, you're live before month-end."
